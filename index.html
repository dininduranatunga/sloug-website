Our role

A stronger forum for Oracle professionals in Sri Lanka.

SLOUG brings professionals, students, partners, and volunteers into one practical community for Oracle events, learning, discussion, and useful local connections.

Connect

Meet Oracle practitioners, customers, partners, architects, DBAs, developers, and students working in Sri Lanka.

Learn

Follow practical sessions on Database, APEX, Cloud, Java, analytics, security, and enterprise applications.

Share

Bring project lessons, implementation notes, and field experience into talks, panels, and workshops.

Learning tracks

Topics that match day-to-day Oracle work.

Membership

Make it easy to stay involved.

Members can find upcoming events, hear about speaker calls, volunteer for sessions, and stay close to the local Oracle network.

  • Early event announcements
  • Access to webinars and meetups
  • Speaker and volunteer opportunities
  • Peer knowledge sharing
